# Stitch Prompts: Digital Marketing Agency Website

Stitch works best when you start with a strong **master prompt** for the look and the first screen, then generate the remaining screens one at a time with short follow-up prompts.

---

## 1. Master prompt (paste first)

Design a premium, desktop-first (1440px) website for a digital marketing agency. Start with the **Home page**.

**Vibe:** minimal, creative, technology-focused, confident. A "growth lab", not a generic template. Dark mode only.

**Colours:** page background near-black #080808; cards #151515; secondary panels #202020; text #F5F5F5; muted text #A5A5A5; hairline borders #303030; single accent electric lime #C0FF3E (used sparingly for CTAs, active states, small highlights and one highlighted word per headline). Text on lime buttons is #101010.

**Typography:** Space Grotesk bold for headlines (very large, tight line-height, slight negative tracking); Inter for body; small uppercase monospace for eyebrow labels and tags.

**Shape and style:** pill buttons, 16 to 24px rounded cards and image frames, 1px hairline borders, generous whitespace, 12-column grid. About 75% solid dark minimal surfaces and 25% selective glassmorphism (translucent white 5% fill, 16px backdrop blur, 1px white 14% border) on the navbar, service cards, stat panels, project tags and the contact form only. Never stack glass layers. Background: faint dark grid fading at the edges with one soft lime radial glow.

**Home page sections, top to bottom:**
1. **Sticky glass navbar:** logo left; links Home, About, Services, Portfolio, Process, Blog, Contact; lime "Let's Talk" button right.
2. **Hero:** mono eyebrow "DIGITAL GROWTH & CREATIVE STRATEGY"; huge headline "We Build Brands. We Create Digital Growth." with "Digital Growth" in lime; one-line supporting paragraph; primary lime button "Start a Project" and outlined button "Explore Our Work"; on the right an abstract dark visual or device mockup with a small floating glass chip showing a metric.
3. **Trusted by:** a row of 6 monochrome placeholder client logos.
4. **Services:** 7 glass cards in a bento grid (SEO, Social Media Marketing, Google and Meta Ads, Content Marketing, Branding and Creative Design, Website Development, Email Marketing), each with an outline icon, short description and arrow link.
5. **About preview:** split layout with large headline, short paragraph, three differentiators and a monochrome team/workspace photo.
6. **Featured projects:** 3 large 16:10 project cards with glass tags (industry, service) over the image.
7. **Results:** 4 glass stat panels with large numbers, lime units and small context labels.
8. **Process:** 5-step connected timeline: Discover, Strategize, Create, Execute, Optimize, each with an icon and one-line description.
9. **Testimonials:** 3 quote cards with name and role.
10. **Closing CTA:** full-width band, "Have a project in mind?", lime button, email and WhatsApp links.
11. **Footer:** solid dark; brand blurb, Pages, Services and Contact columns, newsletter field, social icons, copyright.

Use realistic placeholder copy and clearly generic placeholder logos and metrics. Do not make guaranteed-result claims. Keep body text high-contrast and readable.

## 2. Follow-up prompts (one per screen)

**About**
> Create the About page in the same style. Large editorial headline, company story, mission and vision side by side, 6 values with icons, a 4-column team grid with monochrome portraits (name and role), a milestones timeline, and a closing CTA band.

**Services (overview)**
> Create the Services page. Short hero, then a grid of all 7 services as glass cards with icon, one-line value proposition and "Learn more". End with a process teaser and a CTA band.

**Service detail**
> Create a Service Detail page for Search Engine Optimization. Hero with value proposition, "What's included" checklist, "Who it's for", delivery steps, deliverables list, 3 related project cards, a 4-item FAQ accordion and a CTA.

**Portfolio**
> Create the Portfolio page. Filter pills (All, SEO, Social, Ads, Branding, Web), then a masonry-style grid of 8 project cards with large imagery and glass tags. Add a "Load more" button.

**Case study**
> Create a Case Study detail page. Full-width hero image, client and industry meta row, sections for Challenge, Strategy, Execution and Results (4 stat panels with context), a testimonial, and a "Next project" link.

**Process**
> Create the Process page. Large headline, vertical-and-horizontal connected timeline for the five steps with icons, typical deliverables and indicative durations, a 4-item FAQ, and a CTA.

**Blog**
> Create the Blog page. Featured article card at the top, category pills, a search field, a 3-column grid of article cards (cover image, category, title, excerpt, author, date, read time) and pagination.

**Contact**
> Create the Contact page. Left: headline, short invitation, email, phone, WhatsApp button and office address. Right: glass form panel with Name, Email, Phone, Company, Service (select), Budget (select), Project description and a submit button. Include solid readable inputs, labels above fields, and show one inline error state.

**Mobile**
> Generate the Home page for mobile (390px): compact glass navbar with menu button, stacked CTAs, single-column cards, vertical process timeline, and a full-width closing CTA.

---

## 3. Refinement prompts (use after generating)

- "Reduce the lime usage; keep it only on CTAs, the active nav item and one headline word."
- "Make the headline larger and tighter; increase vertical spacing between sections."
- "Make glass panels more opaque for better text contrast."
- "Make all cards use the same corner radius and border style."
- "Add a subtle lime glow behind the hero visual only."
- "Replace stock-looking imagery with abstract dark, monochrome visuals."
